# Placed Matter

Placed Matter treats space as an active material. Forms do not float for effect;
each one arrives at a precise resting point, held by alignment, counterweight and
the pressure of nearby emptiness. The composition should feel discovered rather
than decorated: a field in which many independent pieces become one instrument
because every interval has been meticulously calibrated.

Colour behaves like a small set of physical inks. Rose, blue, plum, mint and sun
appear as dense, flat deposits against warm paper and deep carbon, never as neon
light or atmospheric gradient. Texture is quiet but present: fibre, registration
drift and minute tonal variation make the work feel printed, handled and refined
over many proofs. The restraint must be the product of deep expertise, not the
absence of detail.

Scale carries the rhythm. One unmistakable anchor is surrounded by repeated
units that change proportion without changing language. Cropped edges, measured
gaps and occasional hairline rules create a patient visual cadence. Nothing is
an icon and nothing illustrates a feature; meaning comes from the relationship
between a whole and its parts, executed with painstaking attention.

Typography, when present, is sparse and structural: one name, one short phrase,
or a clinical reference mark. It belongs to the geometry rather than explaining
it. Large quiet areas remain genuinely quiet. Every baseline, edge and margin
must withstand close inspection as master-level work, with no ornamental filler
and no element added merely to make the canvas feel busy.

The final artifact should feel like a limited-edition technical print for an
imaginary system: exact enough to imply rules, open enough to invite use. Its
craft is visible in reduction — countless decisions compressed into a few forms
that could not be moved without weakening the whole.
